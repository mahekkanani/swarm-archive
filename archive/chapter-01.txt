This is the primary text of the archive.

In the counting of winters, we measure lives — the slow accumulation of seasons
that outlast any single invoice, any single contract, any single promise signed
on perishable paper.

Eight hundred winters is not a duration. It is a monument to persistence.

— Chapter I: The Lapsed Invoice

Every debt that was once recorded is now dust. Every ledger eaten by time.
Only the memory of the obligation remains, passed mouth to ear, season to season.

What Swarm preserves is not the paper but the content.
What the Feed preserves is not the moment but the pointer.
What the batch preserves is not forever — only as long as someone keeps paying.

Remember: renew your postage stamp.
